Straight answers

Questions worth asking.

Is PYR a traditional marketing agency?

No. It is a principal-led boutique focused on digital performance. Paid search is the core service, with analytics, conversion, local SEO and AEO brought in when they improve the outcome.

Do you actually manage the Google Ads account?

Yes. This is not strategy-only consulting. The same senior person diagnosing the problem can execute the account changes, measurement work and optimization.

Why not just hire a PPC specialist?

If all you need is someone to adjust bids and write ads, you probably should. PYR is for situations where the bigger question is why the investment is not performing as well as it should—and what needs to change around the account.

Do you take a percentage of ad spend?

The preferred model is a clear scope and predictable fee based on the complexity and level of work required—not an incentive to increase your media budget.

Do you require a long-term contract?

The goal is to earn the engagement through useful work and measurable improvement. Engagement structure can be discussed based on the starting scope.
