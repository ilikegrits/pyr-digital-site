the promise

gluten free
guarantee

Every cookie, loaf, and pastry at Mirabeau is gluten-free — not as a side note, as the whole point. We bake in a dedicated kitchen so you can eat like you mean it.

Ana takes custom orders for birthdays, showers, office boxes, and “I just need a dozen lavender butters by Thursday.” Pickup in Charlotte. Delivery when you want the cookies to arrive with a little ceremony.
